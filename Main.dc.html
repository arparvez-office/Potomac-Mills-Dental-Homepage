About our practice

A Family-Owned Dental Practice Near Potomac Mills

Our dental services

Complete Dental Care in Woodbridge, VA

Preventive, restorative and cosmetic dentistry under one roof, so your whole family can get everything from routine cleanings to tooth replacement without a referral across town.

Preventive & General

Dental Cleanings & Exams

Twice-yearly checkups with polishing and optional fluoride to prevent cavities and gum disease.

Tooth-Colored Fillings

Composite resin or porcelain fillings that repair decay and blend with your natural teeth.

Periodontal (Gum) Maintenance

Deep cleanings and monitoring that stop periodontal disease from progressing.

Tooth Extractions

Gentle removal of damaged, decayed or crowded teeth, with replacement options planned ahead.

Night Guards & Snore Guards

Custom appliances for teeth grinding (bruxism), clenching and snoring.

Restorative

Dental Implants

Titanium tooth roots that anchor durable, natural-looking replacements for missing teeth.

Dental Crowns

Custom caps that protect cracked or weakened teeth and restore their shape and strength.

Dental Bridges

Fixed bridges anchored to neighboring teeth to fill gaps and restore your bite.

Dentures

Full and partial dentures that help you chew, speak and smile with confidence.

Root Canal Therapy

Endodontic treatment that removes infected pulp to relieve pain and save the natural tooth.

Cosmetic

Invisalign & ClearCorrect

Clear, removable aligners that straighten teeth without metal braces, from a certified provider.

Dental Veneers

Thin custom shells that cover chips, gaps and discoloration on the front of your teeth.

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening that lifts stains from coffee, tea and time for a brighter smile.

Meet your dentist

Dr. Aman Sachdev, DDS

Originally from Long Island, New York, Dr. Sachdev earned his dental degree at the University at Buffalo School of Dental Medicine (SUNY Buffalo) and completed a General Practice Residency at Weill Cornell's New York Hospital Queens. After several years in private practice in Northern Virginia and time at the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), he returned to the chair to lead Potomac Mills Dental.

Outside the office he enjoys time with his wife and children, traveling and discovering new restaurants around Northern Virginia.

Is Potomac Mills Dental accepting new patients?+

Yes. We welcome new patients of all ages from Woodbridge and across Prince William County. Call (703) 878-7969 or complete our new patient form to schedule your first visit.

Which dental insurance plans do you accept?+

We accept Delta Dental, Aetna, Cigna, MetLife, United Concordia, Anthem, Humana, DentaMax, Ameritas, Liberty, Dentegra, GEHA and CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield.

How much is a dental visit without insurance?+

Our Smile Club membership costs $399 a year for patients 14 and older and $299 for children under 14. It includes two exams, two cleanings, annual X-rays, one fluoride treatment and 15% off all treatment.
